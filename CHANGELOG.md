# Changelog

Releases are dated and numbered (semver on the belt as a whole — see
[SENSIBILITIES.md §12](SENSIBILITIES.md#12-release-discipline)). A breaking change is anything that
makes a clone that pulls need to re-run something or change a habit; it bumps the major version
and gets a **Re-run** line here.

## 0.3.0 — shared belt (2026-10-07)

- First release of the shared belt: the kit, the doctor, and the tools that work on anyone's own
  Mac and accounts. Owner-specific tools and research dossiers stay in the owner's private belt.
- `docs/PERSONAL-SETUP.md`: setting the belt up for your own texts, mail and calendar.
